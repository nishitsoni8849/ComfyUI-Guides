## 🧩 Understanding the Parts

ComfyUI is built from small reusable pieces. The guides walk through each one in the order you will actually meet them.

- **The loader boxes** tell ComfyUI which file to read from your computer. If a loader is empty or shows a red outline, it simply means the file it expects is not in the right folder yet.
- **The text boxes** are where your prompt lives. The guides explain which words carry weight and which are effectively ignored by each model.
- **The sampler boxes** control how the picture or video is gradually built up. Two numbers matter most: steps and guidance.
- **The output boxes** decide where your finished result lands and in what format.
- **The wires** connect everything. A wire simply means "the result from this box flows into that box." Nothing more complicated than that.

Once these five ideas click, every workflow on the internet becomes readable.

## ⚙️ Settings That Actually Matter

A typical workflow has dozens of sliders, but only a handful change your result in a meaningful way. The guides separate them clearly.

- Steps: how long the model works on your image or video. More steps usually means more detail, up to a point where it stops improving and just costs extra time.
- Guidance or scale: how strictly the model follows your words. Too low feels loose and drifts from your idea. Too high can look harsh or over-baked.
- Seed: the starting noise. The same seed plus the same settings equals the same result, which makes it the best tool for comparing one change at a time.
- Resolution and frame count: bigger and longer costs more time and memory. The guides include sensible starting values for each of the four models.
- Sampler choice: different methods produce subtly different textures. The guides describe the visual difference rather than the maths behind it.
- Denoise strength: how much freedom the model has to redraw what it is given. This is the key dial when you refine or restyle something that already exists.

## 🛠️ Everyday Workflow Tips

- Change one thing at a time. Adjusting steps, guidance, and seed together tells you nothing about which change caused the difference.
- Save a working baseline. Once a setup gives you a result you like, keep a copy before you start experimenting.
- Write your prompts in plain sentences. Short keyword piles work for some models and confuse others, and the guides say which is which.
- Close other heavy applications while generating video. Long frame counts are demanding, and background programs compete for the same memory.
- Reuse seeds when comparing models. It is the fairest way to see what genuinely differs between MiniMax H3, Wan 2.2, Qwen Image 2.1, and Krea 2.

## ❓ Troubleshooting Common Hiccups

- A loader stays red: the expected file is missing or sitting in the wrong folder. The guide for that model lists the exact location.
- Results look blurry or muddy: usually too few steps, or a resolution the model was not trained around. Raise steps slightly and return to a standard size.
- Results ignore your prompt: guidance is likely too low, or the prompt is packed with conflicting descriptions. Trim it and try again.
- Generation takes forever: frame count and resolution are the usual culprits. Halve one of them and compare the time.
- Nothing downloads: try a different browser, or temporarily pause any download blocker you use.
